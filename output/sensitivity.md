## Reading guide

- Each row is one assumption knob; the bar shows how much the team's title probability moves when the knob is pushed to its low / high edge. The longer the bar, the more title odds depend on that knob.
- Sign convention: `Δ@low` is `prob(low) − prob(baseline)`, so a positive value means the team is **better off** under the lower value of the parameter.
- `rating_sigma_elo` controls how much rating uncertainty is folded into each simulation; pushing it down ⇒ favourites get more confident, pushing it up ⇒ upset tail fattens. Favourites should react oppositely to underdogs.
- Travel / altitude / heat axes typically move only the teams playing in punishing groups. Most teams will see ~0 swing here.
